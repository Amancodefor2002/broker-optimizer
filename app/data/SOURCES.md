# City table sources

`us_cities.csv` (city, state, lat, lon) is generated by
`scripts/build_city_table.py`. Both sources were filtered to the US,
their names normalised, and the two merged into one row per place.

1. **GeoNames** — US places with a population of 500 or more, read
   through the `geonamescache` package. Used first, for city-centre
   coordinates. GeoNames data is licensed under
   [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):
   https://www.geonames.org/
2. **US Cities Database** by Kelvin S. do Prado —
   https://github.com/kelvins/US-Cities-Database — fills in places
   GeoNames doesn't list. Licensed as follows:
